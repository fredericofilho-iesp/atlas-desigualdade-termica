How to read: there is no census comparable across countries, so this map uses only harmonised global data. Values are averages over ~1 km population cells (GHSL) weighted by people, so temperature is measured where people live. Surface temperature is MODIS (1 km, early afternoon) in the hottest three months of each place, which differ across the region (March to May in Mexico and Central America, the dry season in the Amazon and the Brazilian interior, December to February in the Southern Cone). Inequality by income is compared within cities, with each country’s own census data; so far for Brazil, Mexico and Colombia (other units appear without data on that layer).

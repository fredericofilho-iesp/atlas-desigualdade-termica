Rio de Janeiro State

The state’s 92 municipalities.

Click an area on the map to see its values.

How to read: municipal values are built from all census tracts in the state and weighted by people, so temperature is measured where people live, not averaged over forests and farmland. The two “heat gap” indicators show inequality inside each municipality: red means the poorer group (or Black and brown residents) lives in hotter places, blue in cooler ones. In many smaller municipalities the richer households live in the denser, hotter town centre, while poorer ones live in rural, greener areas. Panels below summarise all tracts in the state.
